# Master Thesis

## Title

Modeling the Uptake of Antibodies Targeting the Transferrin Receptor through the Blood Brain Barrier with Ordinary Differential Equations

## Abstract

This thesis is about mathematical modeling in  pharmacokinetics. In particular different approaches to model the uptake of antibodies, which are capable of binding to the transferrin receptor, through the blood brain barrier (BBB) are discussed. The dynamics are described with ordinary differential equations. Multiple simulations are calculated and important properties are proven. Different approaches to simplify the models are discussed and evaluated. One model describes the effects of bispecific antibodies on amyloid-β peptide concentration in the brain and on reticulocyte concentration, and another model describes the dynamics of monoclonal antibodies uptake through the BBB in detail.
